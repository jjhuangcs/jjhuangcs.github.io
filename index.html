Jun-Jie Huang

MPhil (PolyU), PhD, DIC (Imperial College London)

I am an Associate Professor at the College of Computer Science and Technology, National University of Defense Technology (NUDT), Changsha, Hunan, China.

I earned my Ph.D. (2019) at the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Imperial College London, under the supervision of Professor Pier Luigi Dragotti. I received the B.Eng. (Hons) degree (2013) with First Class Honours in Electronic Engineering from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University and the M.Phil. degree (2015) in Electronic and Information Engineering from the same university under the supervision of Professor Wan-Chi Siu.

During 2019 – 2021, I was a postdoctoral research associate at Communications and Signal Processing Group, Electrical and Electronic Engineering Department, Imperial College London, advised by Professor Pier Luigi Dragotti.

My research interests include multi-modal processing, model-based deep learning, and computer vision.

I have published over 70 papers in prestigious journals and conferences, including IEEE TPAMI, TIP, TNNLS, TSP, TCSVT, AAAI, NeurIPS, ACM MM. Full list available on Google Scholar.

I am currently looking for highly motivated Master students. All interested candidates are welcome to apply. Kindly send your CV to me via email.
